Use the `dpm-shape-offer` skill for everything below. Follow it exactly.

The person running this has already researched and scored a crowd with `/dpm:find-niche`. They are new to this and new to Claude Code. Assume they know their crowd and know nothing about offer construction, and never make them feel behind for that.

**Before you say anything, find and read their research.** Look for `research/RESEARCH-*.md` in the project folder. Read the crowd sentence, the verified claims pool, and the quarantine list. If they passed a path in the arguments below, use that instead.

**If there is no research file**, stop. Tell them this step reads from the last one, and to run `/dpm:find-niche` first. Do not offer to shape an offer from a description they type in the chat. That is the exact failure this ordering exists to prevent.

**If the research scored under 9**, say that first, plainly, before anything else. Then ask whether they want to continue anyway or go back and research another candidate. Their call, but they make it knowing.

**If the research is there and it scored well**, open by saying the crowd back to them in one sentence so they can see you actually read it, then say what you already noticed in their research that will matter for the offer: the sharpest complaint quote, the price band the market is already at, the gap the solution landscape left. Then start Step 1.

Hold the whole thing as a conversation. **One question at a time.** Never send a numbered list of questions and wait. The interrogation in Step 2 is meant to be adversarial, so push back when an answer is weak, and say what would have to be true for it to be strong. Do not soften it, and do not invent an advantage they did not claim.

Do not skip the ladder, the AOV arithmetic, or the evidence bank, and do not wait to be asked for any of them. They are the point.

Use the `dpm-niche-research` skill for everything below. Follow it exactly.

The person running this wants to work out what digital product to build. Assume they are new to this and new to Claude Code. Do not assume they know what a "niche" or a "crowd" means in the direct-response sense.

Start the conversation now. Open by asking them what they've got, and give them the three ways in that the skill lists (something they saw, something they already know, something they noticed and don't understand) so they have somewhere to start if they're blank.

Then hold it as a conversation. **One question at a time.** Never send a numbered list of questions. React to what they actually say, offer them concrete reframes rather than asking them to narrow their own thinking, and keep going until you can write the candidate back as a single sentence in the skill's shape.

Do not start any research until they have confirmed that sentence. When they do, tell them plainly what you're about to run and roughly how long it takes, then run the four tracks.

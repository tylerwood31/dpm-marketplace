# Crowd check

Score one candidate crowd against 8 conditions. A candidate that has not been researched gets no score, it gets a list of what to go and find. Guessing here is what makes people build products nobody buys.

## Do the research first

You need real observations, not impressions. At minimum:

1. **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library, country US, "All ads"). Search the crowd's obvious phrases. When you find an advertiser, open their PAGE and view all its ads, because keyword search shows a fraction of what a page runs. Log: page name, number of active ads, oldest "started running" date, destination, price points. **Nobody pays to run a losing ad for months, so an ad live 90+ days is a profitable ad.**
2. **Reddit.** Find the subreddits and note subscriber counts. Search inside them for "how do I", "is there a", "struggling with", "worth it?", "beginner". Complaints are product specs.
3. **A marketplace.** Sort by reviews. Listing age against review count is velocity: a 6-month-old listing with 500 reviews is a monster, a 6-year-old one with 500 is a trickle.

## The 8 conditions

Score each 0, 1 or 2. Say which observation supports each score, and write "no evidence" where you have none rather than inventing a number.

1. **Passionate identity crowd.** They already spend money on this and call themselves by a name.
2. **Buys on impulse.** Comfortable in the $7 to $47 range without a call.
3. **Income or transformation twist.** A bridge from hobby to outcome.
4. **No entrenched operator.** Nobody already running a multi-offer ad machine at this crowd. This is the condition the Ad Library answers.
5. **Deliverable you can make in days.** No certification gate, buildable with AI.
6. **Claim-safe category.** Not health, finance, or anything needing an income guarantee to make sense.
7. **Backend potential.** A natural ladder above the front end.
8. **Visual proof.** The outcome photographs well, so ads write themselves.

## Reading the score

- **13+ with condition 4 scored 2**: build it.
- **13+ with condition 4 scored 0**: real demand, but you are walking into someone else's ad machine. Pick a sharper sub-crowd.
- **9 to 12**: keep it on the list, research the weak conditions properly before committing.
- **Under 9**: next candidate.

Never average across candidates and pick the best of a bad set. Four weak candidates mean the research was too shallow, not that the best weak one is the answer.
